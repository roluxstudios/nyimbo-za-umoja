Getting started takes a minute

The app is installed straight from the download, so your phone may ask for a quick permission the first time.

  1. Tap Download the app and wait for the file to finish.
  2. Open the downloaded file from your notifications or Downloads folder.
  3. If asked, allow installing from your browser, then tap Install.
  4. Open NYIMBO ZA UMOJA and start singing.

Questions, answered

Is the app really free?

Yes. NYIMBO ZA UMOJA is free to download and use.

Do I need internet to read the hymns?

No. Once the app is installed, the hymns work offline.

Why does my phone ask for permission to install?

The app is shared as a direct download instead of through an app store, so Android asks you to allow installs from your browser once. You can switch that permission off again after installing.

Can I read hymns in English?

Some hymns have an English version you can switch to with the toggle at the top of the hymn. More are being added over time.
